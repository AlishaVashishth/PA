Heap follows 2 conditions:
 1. It must be a Complete Binary Tree
 2. It must follow the Heap property.


# There are 2 types of Heaps:

1. Max Heap: 
        In max heap, the parent node is always greater than or equal to its child.

2. Min Heap:
        In min heap, the parent node is always smaller than or equal to its child.


# Difference (Min Heap vs Max Heap):
Feature     Max                      Min

Root:       Maximum                  Minimum
Parent:     greater than child       smaller than child
Used for:   largest element          smallest element
Java:       Priotity Queue reversed  Priority Queue
